OFFICE TRACKER

"Seat booked ≠ actually went to the office."

THE FRICTION

My company's official office seat-booking system tells me which days I booked a seat, but that doesn't necessarily tell me which days I physically went to the office.

THE INSIGHT

I didn't need another attendance system.
I needed a reliable signal of physical presence.

THE BUILD // ZERO-FRICTION PRESENCE SIGNAL AUTOMATED
📱 iPhone Shortcut Automated 2 PM trigger
→
📍 Location Check Foreground GPS ping
→
🎯 ~150m Proximity Office building geofence
→
⚡ PWA Local Store Encrypted client store
→
📊 Attendance History Glanceable monthly pulse

The system checks approximate location around 2 PM and records whether I am physically near the office cluster. No manual check-ins, zero ongoing battery drain.

PRODUCT DECISIONS & TRADE-OFFS

Why the product is designed this way

Why 2 PM?
Captures verified midday presence without battery drain from constant all-day GPS monitoring, and eliminates false positives from morning subway commutes through the area.
Why ~150m?
Accounts for high-rise indoor GPS drift and concrete signal bounce while tightly excluding adjacent cafes and transit stops outside the campus boundary.
What happens when location is inaccurate?
Provides a lightweight 1-tap manual confirmation fallback with local timestamp verification rather than hard blocking the day.
What is the minimum information needed?
A binary presence flag (1/0) and date. Zero invasive continuous tracking, zero biometric storage, zero surveillance bloat.

THE PRODUCT CONCEPT

OPEN FOR DINE-IN NOW

A single, authoritative real-time filter that answers the user's actual question.

THE PROBLEM

Restaurant platforms often answer: "Is this restaurant open?"

But the user's actual question is:
"Can I walk in and eat here right now?"

THE INSIGHT

The problem isn't restaurant discovery.

It is real-time dine-in availability.

PRODUCT THINKING DEEP-DIVE

Hypothesis, MVP & Operational Trade-offs

01 // USER PROBLEM

High Intent, Zero Patience

Diners ready to eat have a 10-minute window before frustration spikes. Multi-app checks create cognitive fatigue.

02 // EXISTING ALTERNATIVES

Reservations vs Walk-ins

Reservation apps serve planned dinners 3 days in advance. Discovery apps serve reviews. Neither serves immediate walk-in demand.

03 // PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

Real-Time Table Signal

Giving restaurants a 1-tap "Accepting Walk-ins" toggle will reduce evening decision latency from 25 min to <3 min.

04 // MVP SCOPE

Zero POS Integration Required

A lightweight hostess app with a binary toggle ("Walk-ins Available: YES/NO") surfaced directly on the consumer map feed.

05 // EDGE CASES

Rush Surges & Ghost Availability

Automatic 20-minute expiry prompt if hostess forgets to toggle; geofenced user confirmation signals when arrival occurs.

06 // WHAT I WOULD MEASURE

Validation Metrics

Search-to-table arrival conversion rate, hostess toggle compliance rate, and walk-in rejection reduction percentage.

07 // WHAT I WOULD BUILD NEXT

Dynamic 15-Minute Table Holds

A 15-minute rolling "On My Way" hold with automatic geofence release if the diner doesn't arrive within the ETA window.

AI PRODUCT THINKING

Designing for Uncertainty and Consumer Trust

User Trust & Fit Confidence
Users don't need a cartoonish novelty filter; they need realistic drape, fabric stretch expectations, and shoulder seam alignment before spending money.
Managing AI Uncertainty
Surface explicit fit confidence intervals rather than pretending the AI model is 100% infallible on complex fabric textures.
User Control & Failure Cases
Provide manual adjustments for torso height, loose vs slim fit preferences, and lighting correction when image input is suboptimal.
Purchase Decision UX
Side-by-side comparison with the shopper's existing wardrobe items to eliminate return friction and buyer remorse.
