03 // DEEP DIVE CASE STUDY TREASURY REGULATORY TECHNOLOGY (RLT)

Compressing 40 Minutes of Fragile Manual Reporting Into 12 Seconds.

PREVIOUS STATE 30–40 min Manual spreadsheet extraction & repeated intervention
→
AUTOMATED PIPELINE 12 sec Deterministic, audit-ready automated delivery
01

THE PROBLEM

Regulatory Liquidity Testing (RLT) required treasury analysts to pull disparate ledger snapshots, manually resolve column mismatches, and copy-paste formulas across fragmented spreadsheets under stringent market close deadlines.

02

THE APPROACH

Re-engineered the pipeline around declarative data contracts, autonomous data validation microservices, and unified stream transformation that converts raw transaction logs into validated regulatory balance sheets.

03

THE RESULT

Eliminated manual touchpoints, achieved 99.5% runtime reduction, guaranteed repeatable audit compliance, and liberated analysts from operational toil to strategic liquidity forecasting.
