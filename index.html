PRODUCT THINKING DEEP DIVE

How We Scaled Mango Factory From Insight to Commercial Scale

01 // THE OPPORTUNITY

The Friction in the Market

Indian retail fruit supply chains are plagued by artificial carbide chemical ripening, opaque middlemen, and unpredictable quality. Customers had no reliable way to get authentic, chemical-free farm mangoes directly at peak flavor.

02 // THE PRODUCT

What We Built & Offered

An end-to-end direct-to-consumer farm-to-table experience: curated heritage seasonal harvests (Alphonso, Banganapalli, Raspuri, Kesar), orchard-origin traceability, customized cushion-cell protective packaging, and intuitive digital ordering.

03 // CUSTOMER UNDERSTANDING

Deep User Segmentation

We identified 3 core customer cohorts: Authenticity seekers craving childhood farm sweetness, health-conscious parents avoiding carbide chemicals, and premium corporate/personal seasonal gifters where unboxing presentation is paramount.

04 // BUILDING & EXECUTION

Taking It 0-to-1 As a Team

We orchestrated direct partnerships with regional heritage orchards, structured harvest-time sorting and quality inspection protocols, built ventilated protective packaging workflows, and established rapid direct fulfillment channels under strict perishability timeframes.

05 // ITERATION

Learning from Real Operations

We iterated internal box dividers based on early transit vibration data. Introduced stage-by-stage ripeness guides inside every box ("Eat 1-2 today, remaining in 2-3 days"). Built seasonal pre-booking cohorts for recurring customers.

06 // BUSINESS IMPACT

Commercial Validation

Achieved ₹1 Cr+ in business revenue driven by high customer retention, organic word-of-mouth recommendations, and unit-economic discipline across seasonal harvest runs.

07 // PRODUCT LEARNINGS

What Building Mango Factory Taught Us About Product Management

1. Operations IS Product: In physical ventures, delivery delays and packaging failures are product defects. You cannot separate software from fulfillment.
2. Extreme Urgency & Customer Trust: Seasonal perishable windows demand ruthless prioritization, transparent customer communication, and zero room for quality compromise.
3. Product Judgment: True product thinking is the synthesis of deep customer empathy, physical operational reality, and commercial viability.

02 // PERSONAL PRODUCT PWA · AUTOMATION · GEOLOCATION

OFFICE TRACKER

"Seat booked ≠ actually went to the office."

01 // THE FRICTION

My company's official seat-booking portal tells me which days I booked a seat, but it does not reliably tell me which days I actually went to the office. Remembering past physical presence for monthly records was manual, fuzzy, and error-prone.

02 // THE INSIGHT

I didn't need another heavy corporate dashboard or battery-killing continuous background GPS tracking. I just needed a single, reliable location-based presence signal captured once during peak office hours.

SYSTEM MECHANISM AUTOMATED PRESENCE CHECK
⏰
iOS SHORTCUT
Triggered automatically around 2:00 PM on weekdays
→
📍
LOCATION CHECK
Fetches current device GPS coordinates
→
⭕
~150m GEOFENCE
Validates proximity to office building radius
→
📱
PWA STORE
Silently logs physical presence status locally
→
📊
CALENDAR LOG
Generates monthly verified attendance history

How it works: At 2:00 PM, an iOS Shortcut triggers a single foreground GPS check. If the device is within ~150 meters of the office coordinates, it passes a verified presence token to the lightweight Progressive Web App, logging the day automatically.

PRODUCT THINKING

Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

01

Why 2:00 PM?

Midday is when physical office presence is most certain. It avoids false positives from morning subway commutes through the neighborhood and eliminates the risk of missing someone who leaves by 5:00 PM.

02

Why ~150 Meters?

Urban high-rises cause concrete GPS drift up to 80m. A 150m radius encompasses the entire building footprint while strictly excluding nearby subway transit corridors and perimeter cafes.

03

Inaccurate Location Handling

If GPS accuracy is degraded (error > 100m), the shortcut falls back to a 1-tap interactive prompt: "Are you at the office today?" with local timestamp recording rather than failing silently.

04

Minimum Useful Information

Only a binary flag (1 / 0) and date are recorded. No continuous path tracking, no battery drain, and complete personal privacy by design.

PRODUCT OUTCOME

"A manual check became something I stopped thinking about."

Built as a personal product to solve my own real daily friction, creating a frictionless, reliable record of physical office attendance.

03 // PERSONAL PRODUCT PRODUCTIVITY · PERSONAL WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

JOB TRACKER

"A job search is not a simple to-do list. It is an active multi-stage funnel with high cognitive load."

01 // THE PROBLEM

Job applications quickly become fragmented across company career pages, LinkedIn, referrals, recruiter conversations, emails, interview stages, and follow-ups. It becomes difficult to remember where I applied, what stage I reached, what I need to follow up on, and what I learned from previous rounds.

02 // THE PRODUCT

Job Tracker creates a single unified system for managing the entire job-search journey. Instead of static spreadsheet rows, it structures each opportunity into a dynamic lifecycle with proactive follow-up reminders and debrief intelligence.

The Core Architecture: Replaces messy unstructured notes with an active lifecycle funnel. Each application tracks dates, stage gates, interviewer profiles, preparation debriefs, and automated follow-up cadences.

PRODUCT THINKING

Designing for Cognitive Relief & Funnel Clarity

01

Information Architecture

Grouped application data into hierarchical cards: Company Context, Role Requirements, Timeline Milestones, Recruiter Contacts, and Interview Debrief Notes.

02

Stage Gate Lifecycle

Modeled applications as state machines rather than static lists. Moving an opportunity to 'Interview Scheduled' automatically generates prep checklists and prompts for follow-up reminders.

03

Reducing Cognitive Load

Instead of manually scanning dozens of open applications daily, the dashboard highlights only urgent actions needed today: pending follow-ups, upcoming interviews, and inactive lead archives.

04

Making the Search Measurable

Visualizes stage conversion rates (e.g., Applied → Screened → Final Round), pinpointing whether friction lies in resume screening or final presentation stages.

04 // PRODUCT CONCEPT HOME · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE · ASSET MEMORY

OWNLY

"What if your home had a memory?"

01 // THE SCATTERED REALITY

People accumulate purchases, invoices, warranties, manuals, service records, and product details — but these are scattered across email, WhatsApp, Amazon, photos, and physical drawers. When a washing machine leaks or an AC needs service, finding the manual or warranty is painful.

02 // THE SYNTHESIS

OWNLY: Your home's memory layer. A centralized system mapping: What I Own → Where it is → When I bought it → Warranty status → Service history → Documents.

01

Spatial Memory

Organize assets by room (Kitchen, Living Room, Master Bedroom) so locating an item's documentation matches how you physically experience your home.

02

Proactive Warranty Clocks

Automated countdowns notify you 30 days before manufacturer warranties expire, giving you a chance to request maintenance before coverage lapses.

03

1-Click Manuals & Service

Auto-matches model numbers with official manufacturer PDF manuals and authorized service contact portals in one tap.

04

Privacy by Design

Household asset records remain private, client-encrypted, and locally backed up with optional selective sharing for roommates or property managers.

05 // PRODUCT CONCEPT RESTAURANTS · REAL-TIME AVAILABILITY · SPONTANEOUS INTENT

DINE-IN NOW

"It's 8:17 PM. I want dinner."

01 // THE FRAGMENTED JOURNEY

The actual user question is not restaurant discovery, but: "Can I walk into this restaurant and eat there right now?" Today's journey: Google Maps says "Open" → Swiggy shows delivery → Dineout offers a 9:30 PM slot → Call restaurant → "Sorry, no walk-ins tonight."

02 // THE BREAKTHROUGH

OPEN FOR DINE-IN NOW. A single real-time binary status switch that hostesses can flip in 1 second, showing spontaneous walk-in table availability on a live map within a 15-minute radius.

01

1-Tap Hostess Switch

Zero complex POS integration required. Hostesses toggle a simple green/red switch on a tablet at the host stand whenever immediate walk-in tables open up.

02

15-Minute Dynamic Hold

Diners can claim a 'Head Over Now' 15-minute table hold token, giving restaurants committed foot traffic while preventing phantom no-shows.

03

Automatic State Expiry

To prevent stale data, the "Tables Open" status auto-expires after 45 minutes unless the restaurant explicitly re-confirms with a single tap.

04

Spontaneous Intent Filter

Only shows restaurants within immediate walking/driving range (max 15 min travel time) to ensure the availability matches physical travel feasibility.

06 // PRODUCT CONCEPT AI · PRODUCT EXPERIENCE · E-COMMERCE UX

AI VIRTUAL TRY-ON

"Will this actually look good on me?"

01 // THE AI TRUST GAP

E-commerce returns remain as high as 30–40% because studio product photos on models fail to show how fabric drapes on diverse real human bodies. However, hallucinated or overly flattered AI generations destroy customer trust.

02 // THE EXPERIENCE DESIGN

Designing Trust Around AI Uncertainty. A seamless try-on journey: Upload photo → Select garment → Realistic drape mapping with fit confidence indicators → Side-by-side wardrobe comparison.

01

Fit Confidence Indicators

Instead of falsely claiming 100% precision, the UI displays explicit stretch, drape, and length fit confidence scores based on user height and measurements.

02

Side-by-Side Comparison

Allows users to toggle between multiple outfit combinations instantly, turning speculative browsing into confident purchase decisions.

03

Lighting & Pose Fallbacks

Detects poor upload lighting or extreme angles and provides gentle guided framing cues before running diffusion inference to ensure high-fidelity drape renders.

04

Expectation Calibration

Frames AI output as a realistic silhouette approximation rather than a guaranteed tailoring snapshot, protecting brand integrity and reducing post-purchase return regret.
